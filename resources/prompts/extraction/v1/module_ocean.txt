MODULE FOCUS: OCEAN
- Master level: MBL number, carrier/SCAC, booking number, vessel and voyage,
  POL, POD, place of receipt/delivery, ETD/ETA vs ATD/ATA, freight terms.
- House level: each HBL as a separate record with its own shipper, consignee,
  notify party, packages, gross weight, volume and description.
- Containers: number, seal(s), type/size (raw + normalized), packages, gross
  weight, tare/VGM if shown, volume. One record per container, linked to the
  correct HBL only when the document shows the link.
- Routing: each leg as a separate record (vessel/voyage, load/discharge port,
  dates with estimated and actual kept apart).
- ocean_import: consignee/notify are usually the local parties; ocean_export:
  shipper is usually the local party. Role mapping and local-party rules come
  from {{FIELD_RULES}}; do not assume.
