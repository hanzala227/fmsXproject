MODULE FOCUS: AIR
- Master level: MAWB number, airline, flight number(s) and date(s), airport of
  departure and destination (IATA), pieces, gross weight, volume, rate class,
  handling information, declared values if shown.
- House level: each HAWB as a separate record with its own parties, pieces,
  weights and cargo description.
- Weights: gross, volumetric and chargeable are different values with units.
  Never replace a printed chargeable weight with a calculated one.
- Routing: each flight leg as its own record.
- Role mapping for import vs export comes from {{FIELD_RULES}}; do not assume.
