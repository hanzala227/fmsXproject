1. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & ACCOUNTING ENGINE OVERVIEW
====================================================================================================

The accounting subsystem of FreightX / FMS 2.0 operates as an automated double-entry general ledger
and freight-billing engine. It is strictly coupled to operational freight events (Ocean Import/Export,
Air Import/Export, Trucking) while enforcing segregation of duties through a four-eyes verification
gateway.

1.1 Underlying Schema & Relational Integrity:
- `charges`: Primary operational cost and revenue accumulation table. Stores rating lines linked
  polymorphically via `chargeable_type` and `chargeable_id` with `type` enum ('AR', 'AP', 'DC_NOTE').
- `invoices`: Primary commercial billing document header table. Holds document identity (`document_type`:
  'INV', 'DN', 'CN'), `type` ('AR', 'AP'), status enums ('DRAFT', 'POSTED', 'PAID', 'PARTIAL', 'VOID'),
  and four-eyes approval status (`verification_status`: 'Pending Verification', 'Verified', 'Rejected').
- `invoice_lines`: Itemized line charges detailing rates, quantities, currency, and line types.
- `accounting_payments`: Primary cash and bank ledger table. Stores incoming customer settlements
  (`type`: 'RECEIVED') and outgoing carrier disbursements (`type`: 'MADE') linked to parent invoices.
- `document_sequences`: High-concurrency sequence allocation table enforcing pessimistic row locks
  and uniqueness for commercial billing documents.
- `currencies`: Master table for foreign exchange conversions against the functional currency (USD).
- `gl_accounts`: Standard Chart of Accounts mapping 1000 (Cash/Bank), 1200 (AR), 2000 (AP),
  4000 (Revenue), and 5000 (Expenses).

1.2 Multi-Currency & Functional Currency Model:
- Functional Reporting Currency: USD ($).
- All transactions are recorded in their original transaction currency (e.g. USD, EUR, AED, PKR)
  preserving commercial contract fidelity.
- Historical Exchange Rate Immutability: When an invoice or payment is created, its active exchange
  rate is stamped into an immutable `exchange_rate` decimal column on the record. Future fluctuations
  or administrative modifications in master currency tables do not alter historical posted general
  ledger entries or financial statements.
- Financial Statement Normalization: Reports aggregate base currency amounts by evaluating:
  `$baseAmount = ($rate != 1.0) ? round($amount / $rate, 2) : $amount;`
  ensuring uniform base currency aggregation across all foreign transactions.

2. END-TO-END FLOW OF ACCOUNTING TRANSACTIONS
====================================================================================================

The financial lifecycle follows seven discrete, sequential phases from operational inception
to final trial balance closure:

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 1: Operational Shipment Creation & Charge Rating
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. A logistics shipment is initialized across any supported operational module:
   - Ocean Import: `App\Models\OceanImport`
   - Ocean Export: `App\Models\OceanExport`
   - Air Import:   `App\Models\AirImport` (with IATA volumetric weight divisor algorithms applied)
   - Air Export:   `App\Models\AirExport`
   - Trucking:     `App\Models\TruckShipment`
2. Operational charges are assigned to the shipment:
   - Receivable charges (`type = 'AR'`): Freight, customs, documentation, handling, terminal fees.
     These define the expected revenue receivable from the billing trade partner.
   - Payable charges (`type = 'AP'`): Ocean linehaul, air freight, trucker vehicle hire, port tariffs.
     These define expected direct operational liabilities payable to carriers/vendors.
3. Accounting State: No general ledger entry exists. Operational charges remain purely operational
   commitments with `is_invoiced = 0`. Profit summary screens compute preliminary operational gross
   margins (`AR Charges - AP Charges`), but financial reports (Income Statement, Balance Sheet) remain
   unaffected.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 2: Commercial Document Generation & Document Sequencing
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. From the shipment rating console or accounting billing console, invoices are generated.
2. The `DocumentSequenceService` generates a gapless, collision-free invoice number:
   - Executes inside an isolated database transaction.
   - Applies `lockForUpdate()` on the `document_sequences` row for the tenant and document type.
   - Generates sequential identifiers: `INV-000001-A` (Original Invoice), `DN-000001-A` (Debit Note),
     `CN-000001-A` (Credit Note).
   - If multiple billing documents originate from the same shipment, the service assigns sequential
     group letters (e.g., `INV-000001-A` for Customer AR, `INV-000001-B` for Carrier AP).
3. Associated operational charges are flagged `is_invoiced = 1` and stamped with `invoice_no` and
   `invoice_date` to prevent duplicate billing.
4. Initial Document State:
   - `status`: 'DRAFT'
   - `verification_status`: 'Pending Verification'
5. Financial State: Strict zero-leakage isolation. Draft documents are ignored by financial reports.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 3: Four-Eyes Verification Queue Gateway
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Junior operational staff generate invoices, but cannot authorize commercial posting.
2. Invoices enter the Verification Queue (`/accounting/invoice/verify-queue`).
3. An authorized manager or financial accountant reviews the document:
   - Commercial rate verification.
   - Customer credit terms and billing entity verification.
   - Currency and exchange rate verification.
4. Approval Action:
   - Updating `verification_status` to 'Verified' triggers automatic status promotion to 'POSTED'.
   - The document is stamped with `verified_by` (User ID) and `verified_at` (Timestamp).
5. Financial Recognition Trigger:
   - Upon transition to 'POSTED', the invoice enters the General Ledger.
   - AR Invoices immediately recognize Operating Revenue (Account 4000) and Accounts Receivable (Account 1200).
   - AP Carrier Bills immediately recognize Operating Expenses (Account 5000) and Accounts Payable (Account 2000).

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 4: Commercial Adjustments & Credit Note Safeguards
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. If cargo is damaged, delayed, or billed incorrectly, a Credit Note (`document_type = 'CN'`) is issued.
2. Controller-Level Boundary Guard:
   - The system checks: `(float) $cn->total_amount <= (float) $originalInvoice->total_amount`.
   - Any attempt to issue a Credit Note exceeding the parent invoice is blocked with HTTP 422:
     "Prohibited: Credit Note amount exceeds original invoice amount."
3. Accounting Recognition:
   - Credit Notes operate as Contra-Revenue, NOT cash outflows.
   - In the Income Statement: `CASE WHEN document_type = 'CN' THEN -total_amount ELSE total_amount END`.
   - In Accounts Receivable: The Credit Note reduces the gross customer balance directly.
   - Bank balances are untouched. Zero cash movement occurs.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 5: Cash & Bank Settlement (Receipts & Disbursements)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Customer Payments (`type = 'RECEIVED'`):
   - Initiated via `/accounting/payments` or payment modals.
   - High-Concurrency Pessimistic Row Lock:
     ```php
     DB::transaction(function() {
         $inv = Invoice::where('id', $id)->lockForUpdate()->first();
         if ($paymentAmount > $inv->balance_amount) {
             throw new Exception("Payment exceeds outstanding balance.");
         }
         // Post payment and reduce balance atomically
     });
     ```
   - Serializes concurrent payment submissions, preventing double-spend and negative balances.
   - An overpayment is rejected with HTTP 422: "Payment amount exceeds remaining invoice balance."
2. Document Status Transition:
   - If `paid_amount < total_amount`: status becomes 'PARTIAL'.
   - If `paid_amount == total_amount`: status becomes 'PAID' (Balance: $0.00).
3. Supplier AP Disbursements (`type = 'MADE'`):
   - Payments made to ocean carriers, airlines, truckers, and port authorities.
   - Decreases Cash/Bank (Account 1000) and relieves Accounts Payable (Account 2000).
   - Completely discharges the liability to $0.00 balance.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 6: Foreign Exchange (FOREX) Realization
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Multi-currency transactions track carrying book value versus settlement value.
2. Invoicing Phase:
   - Foreign invoice (e.g., €3,000 EUR at rate 1.10 USD/EUR) booked at carrying value = $3,300.00 USD.
3. Settlement Phase:
   - Customer settles in EUR, but the bank wire realizes at active spot rate 1.12 USD/EUR = $3,360.00 USD.
   - Math:
     * Carrying Value Relieved: $3,300.00 USD
     * Actual Bank Cash Deposited: $3,360.00 USD
     * Realized Foreign Exchange Gain: $3,360.00 - $3,300.00 = +$60.00 USD.
4. Conversely, if PKR 600,000 billed at 280 PKR/USD ($2,142.86 USD) settles at 285 PKR/USD ($2,105.26 USD):
   - Realized Foreign Exchange Loss = $2,105.26 - $2,142.86 = -$37.59 USD.
5. Accounting Integrity: The customer receivable is relieved at 100% of its carrying book value,
   clearing the AR balance to $0.00 without leaving orphaned foreign fractional cents.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 7: Financial Reporting & General Ledger Closure
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Real-time dynamic aggregation compiles:
   - Trial Balance: Debits vs Credits across all accounts.
   - Income Statement: Net Revenue (Gross Revenue - Credit Notes) - Direct & Operating Expenses.
   - Balance Sheet: Assets (Cash + AR) = Liabilities (AP) + Equity (Retained Earnings).
   - Aging Reports: Categorizes open unpaid invoices into Current (0-30), 31-60, 61-90, 90+ buckets.

3. FINANCIAL STATEMENT RECONCILIATION & DOUBLE-ENTRY PROOF
====================================================================================================

Every financial report in FreightX derives from the same underlying posted transactions.
Mathematical reconciliation is governed by five immutable accounting identities:

3.1 Trial Balance Equilibrium:
    Total Debits == Total Credits (Variance: $0.00)
    Every posted invoice, payment, credit note, and expense creates balancing dual entries:
    - Cash Receipt: Debit Cash (1000), Credit Accounts Receivable (1200).
    - Invoice Post: Debit Accounts Receivable (1200), Credit Operating Revenue (4000).
    - Credit Note: Debit Contra-Revenue (4000), Credit Accounts Receivable (1200).
    - Supplier Bill: Debit Operating Expense (5000), Credit Accounts Payable (2000).
    - Supplier Pay: Debit Accounts Payable (2000), Credit Cash (1000).

3.2 Balance Sheet Equation:
    Total Assets == Total Liabilities + Total Equity (Variance: $0.00)
    - Total Assets = Cash and Bank Balances + Accounts Receivable.
    - Total Liabilities = Accounts Payable.
    - Total Equity = Opening Capital + Retained Earnings (Current Period Net Operating Income).

3.3 Income Statement Identity:
    Net Operating Income == Operating Revenue (less Credit Notes) - Operating Expenses
    Directly links to the Retained Earnings entry on the Balance Sheet.

3.4 Accounts Receivable Continuity:
    Closing AR == Opening AR + Posted Invoices - Approved Credit Notes - Received Payments
    Every dollar reduction in customer balances corresponds to either a cash receipt or an authorized CN.

3.5 Accounts Payable Continuity:
    Closing AP == Opening AP + Posted Supplier Bills - Approved Supplier Payments
    Every dollar reduction in carrier liability corresponds directly to a verified bank payout.

4. THE THREE DASHBOARDS: ARCHITECTURE, ROLE SEGREGATION & DATA SYNCHRONIZATION
====================================================================================================

FreightX implements three strictly partitioned dashboards catering to distinct stakeholders:

====================================================================================================
4.1 SUPERADMIN DASHBOARD (`/super-admin`)
====================================================================================================
- Target Persona: SaaS Platform Owner / System Administrator.
- Controller: `App\Http\Controllers\SuperAdmin\DashboardController`
- Access Control: Middleware `['auth', 'verified', 'super-admin']`. Only users with `role = 'SuperAdmin'`
  and `company_id = NULL` are permitted access.
- Functional Scope:
  * Platform-wide multi-tenant oversight.
  * Total registered companies, active companies, suspended companies, and trial companies.
  * System operational status: database connection health, storage utilization, background queue jobs.
  * Company lifecycle management: Tenant provisioning, user limits, storage quota enforcement,
    and instantaneous company suspension/activation toggles.
- Financial Isolation Safeguard:
  * The SuperAdmin dashboard strictly displays platform metadata and operational shipment volumes.
  * Tenant-level commercial financials (individual customer invoices, banking cash balances, customer
    profit margins) are NEVER mixed into platform totals.
  * Guarantees total confidentiality and multi-tenant data isolation.

====================================================================================================
4.2 FORWARDER / COMPANY ADMIN DASHBOARD (`/dashboard`)
====================================================================================================
- Target Persona: Freight Forwarding Company Executive, Operations Director, Financial Controller.
- Controller: `App\Http\Controllers\DashboardController`
- Access Control: Middleware `['auth', 'verified', 'role:Admin,User']`. Scoped strictly to the
  authenticated user's `company_id` via global Eloquent tenant scopes (`BelongsToTenant`).
- Functional Scope:
  * Real-Time KPI Cards:
    - Total Profit: Accrued AR charges less AP charges across the active operational ledger.
    - Total Volume: Aggregated shipment counts across Ocean Import, Ocean Export, Air Import, Air Export,
      and Trucking.
    - Active Customers vs. Lost Customers: Computed by evaluating transaction recency (6 vs 12 months).
  * Operational To-Do List:
    - Displays active shipments requiring urgent operational intervention (Pre-alerts, Customs Holds,
      Arrival Notices, Invoicing Pending).
    - Deep-links directly to operational editing interfaces (`/ocean-import/{id}/edit`, etc.).
  * Analytics & Forecasting Area Charts (`/dashboard/chart-data`):
    - Live multi-month billing and expense visualization.
    - Dynamically plots historical revenue vs carrier costs, displaying live operational gross margins.
- Accounting Synchronization:
  * The Forwarder Admin dashboard reads directly from the underlying `charges` and `invoices` tables.
  * Posting a new invoice or verifying a credit note instantly updates the financial KPIs upon page refresh
    or AJAX polling.

====================================================================================================
4.3 CUSTOMER PORTAL DASHBOARD (`/client-portal/client` and `/portal/dashboard`)
====================================================================================================
- Target Persona: Commercial Importer / Exporter (The Freight Forwarder's Client).
- Controller: `App\Http\Controllers\ClientController` & `CustomerPortal\DashboardController`
- Access Control: Middleware `['auth', 'verified', 'check-status', 'customer-access']` and `ClientMiddleware`.
  Requires `user->role === 'Customer' || user->role === 'client'` AND a non-null `trade_partner_id`.
- Functional Scope:
  * Customer-specific view: Displays ONLY shipments, invoices, tracking events, and documents where
    `customer_id == auth()->user()->trade_partner_id`.
  * Commercial Self-Service:
    - Review posted invoices and credit notes (`/client-portal/invoices`).
    - Download verified invoice PDFs.
    - Review outstanding customer ledger balance.
    - Live shipment milestone tracking (Departure, In-Transit, Customs Release, Delivered).
- Security & Anti-Leakage Safeguards:
  * Zero Margin Exposure: Customers CANNOT see carrier costs, supplier bills, forwarder buy-rates,
    or internal profit margins. The entire Accounts Payable engine is stripped from customer responses.
  * Role Boundary Enforcement: Attempting to navigate to Forwarder views (`/dashboard`, `/accounting/*`,
    `/ocean-import/*`) immediately intercepts the session via `RoleMiddleware` and redirects the user
    back to `/client-portal/client`.
  * Inherent IDOR Immunity: Directly manipulating URL identifiers (e.g. attempting to view another
    customer's invoice at `/client-portal/invoices/999`) executes tenant and trade-partner scoped queries
    that return a strict HTTP 404 Not Found response.
