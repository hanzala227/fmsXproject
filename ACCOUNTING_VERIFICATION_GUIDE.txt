2. END-TO-END FINANCIAL ARCHITECTURE & TRANSACTION LIFECYCLE
====================================================================================================

The financial lifecycle in FMS 2.0 follows a strict 5-stage progressive flow:

  [STAGE 1: OPERATIONS & QUOTATION]
         │
         ▼
  Shipment Booking / File Created (Air, Ocean, Land)
  Charges Entered:
    ├── AR Charges (Receivables: Freight Sell, Handling, Customs Clearance, Doc Fee, Fuel Surcharge)
    └── AP Charges (Payables: Carrier Ocean/Air Freight Cost, Trucking Drayage, Port Storage, Wharfage)
  Formulas Applied:
    * Amount = Rate × Qty × Rate of Exchange (ROE)
    * Tax = Amount × (VAT% / 100)
    * Line Total = Amount + Tax
         │
         ▼
  [STAGE 2: BILLING & INVOICE GENERATION]
         │
         ▼
  InvoiceGenerationService processes un-invoiced charges (`is_invoiced = false`):
    * Charges are grouped by Trade Partner (`bill_to_id`) and PR Category (`AR` vs `AP`).
    * Document Type Determination:
        - If Partner is Overseas Agent / Forwarder AND Charge is AR  ──> Generates DEBIT NOTE (DN)
        - If Partner is Overseas Agent / Forwarder AND Charge is AP  ──> Generates CREDIT NOTE (CN)
        - If Partner is Client / Shipper / Consignee AND Charge is AR ──> Generates COMMERCIAL INVOICE (INV)
        - If Partner is Carrier / Vendor / Trucker AND Charge is AP  ──> Generates VENDOR BILL / INVOICE (INV/AP)
    * DocumentSequenceService generates human-readable unique sequence numbers:
      Format: `[PREFIX]-[BRANCH_CODE]-[YEAR_MONTH]-[SEQUENCE][GROUP_LETTER]` (e.g. `INV-DH-2611-0001A`)
    * Invoice Header & Lines are saved.
    * Charges updated: `is_invoiced = true`, `invoice_no = [NEW_INV_NO]`, `invoice_date = now()`.
    * Initial State: `status = DRAFT`, `verification_status = Pending Verification`.
         │
         ▼
  [STAGE 3: VERIFICATION & POSTING WORKFLOW]
         │
         ▼
  Internal Control / Dual Authorization:
    * Head of Accounts / Senior Billing Manager reviews document in `/accounting/verify-queue`.
    * Action: Verify Document.
    * State Updated:
        `verification_status = 'Verified'`
        `status = 'POSTED'`
        `verified_by = User ID`, `verified_at = Timestamp`
    * The invoice is now locked against junior editing and becomes an active ledger entry.
         │
         ▼
  [STAGE 4: CUSTOMER PORTAL & ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE REFLECTION]
         │
         ▼
  Customer logs into Customer Portal (`/portal/invoices`):
    * Strict Multi-tenant Scope: `company_id = auth()->user()->company_id`.
    * Strict Customer Scope: `bill_to_id = auth()->user()->trade_partner_id`.
    * Strict Status Filter: `status IN ('POSTED', 'PARTIAL', 'PAID')`.
    * Customer views real-time Billed Amount, Paid Amount, Balance Due, and PDF breakdown.
         │
         ▼
  [STAGE 5: CASH RECEIPT, SETTLEMENT & RECONCILIATION]
         │
         ▼
  Cash/Wire/Check received from customer (`/accounting/payment/receive`):
    * AccountingPayment record created in `payments` table (`type = 'RECEIVED'`).
    * Linked to `invoice_id`.
    * `syncInvoiceBalance()` triggers automatically:
        - `paid_amount = SUM(active payments on this invoice)`
        - `balance_amount = total_amount - paid_amount`
        - If `balance_amount == 0`: `status = 'PAID'`
        - If `balance_amount > 0 && paid_amount > 0`: `status = 'PARTIAL'`
    * Customer Portal & Super Admin dashboards reflect instantaneous settlement.

3. MODULE-BY-MODULE CALCULATION AUDIT & FORMULA VERIFICATION
====================================================================================================

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 3.1: AIR FREIGHT (Air Export & Air Import)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Location: `app/Services/AirExportService.php`, `app/Http/Controllers/AirExportController.php`

1. CHARGEABLE WEIGHT (IATA Standard Formula):
   In air cargo, airlines bill on whichever is greater: Gross Weight vs Volumetric Weight.
   - Metric Formula:
     Volumetric Weight (KGS) = (Length cm × Width cm × Height cm) ÷ 6,000
   - Imperial Formula:
     Volumetric Weight (LBS) = (Length in × Width in × Height in) ÷ 166
   - Chargeable Weight = MAX(Actual Gross Weight, Volumetric Weight)

2. CHARGE LINE CALCULATION:
   For every charge item:
     Amount (Local Currency) = Rate × Quantity × Rate of Exchange (ROE)
     VAT / Tax Amount        = Amount × (Tax% ÷ 100)
     Total Line Amount       = Amount + Tax Amount

3. PROFIT & LOSS FORMULA (Shipment Level):
   Total Revenue = SUM(All AR Charges where type = 'AR')
   Total Cost    = SUM(All AP Charges where type IN ('AP', 'origin_cost'))
   Total Profit  = Total Revenue - Total Cost
   Gross Profit Margin % = (Total Profit ÷ Total Revenue) × 100
   Markup on Cost %      = (Total Profit ÷ Total Cost) × 100

   * AUDIT FIX NOTICE:
     In `AirExportController.php` line 1121, the system previously had:
     `$profitMargin = ($totalCost / $totalCost) * 100;` (Which was always 100.00%).
     This has been corrected to:
     `$profitMargin = ($totalProfit / $totalCost) * 100;` (Markup on Cost).

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 3.2: OCEAN FREIGHT (Ocean Import & Ocean Export)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Location: `app/Services/OceanImportService.php`, `app/Http/Controllers/OceanImportController.php`

1. CONTAINER BASIS (FCL - Full Container Load):
   - Rates quoted per container unit (e.g., 20GP, 40GP, 40HQ, 45HQ, Reefer).
   - Quantity = Number of containers.
   - Amount = Rate × Container Count.

2. CBM / MEASUREMENT BASIS (LCL - Less than Container Load):
   - Rated on Revenue Ton (W/M - Weight or Measurement):
   - 1 CBM (Cubic Meter) = 1,000 KGS. Whichever is greater determines the billing ton.

3. CHARGES & SURCHARGES:
   - Freight: Ocean Freight (O/F), BAF (Bunker Adjustment Factor), CAF (Currency Adjustment Factor).
   - Origin / Destination: THC (Terminal Handling Charge), Wharfage, Documentation Fee, Customs.
   - Demurrage & Detention:
     Storage Start Date, Storage End Date, Empty Confirmed Date, Free Time (Days).

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 3.3: LAND TRANSPORT / TRUCKING
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Location: `app/Services/TruckShipmentService.php`, `app/Http/Controllers/TruckShipmentController.php`

1. BASIS:
   - Flat Trip Rate, Per Mile Rate, Weight CWT (Hundredweight / 100 lbs), or Per Pallet.
   - Accessorials: Fuel Surcharge (FSC %), Stop-off Fee, Driver Detention (Hourly), Liftgate.
   - Calculation: Linehaul Amount + (Linehaul × FSC%) + Accessorials = Total Trucking Cost.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 3.4: INVOICING & FINANCIAL ARITHMETIC
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Location: `app/Http/Controllers/InvoiceController.php` (`syncInvoiceTotals`)

The financial integrity of every invoice is enforced by strict linear mathematics:
   Lines Sum        = SUM(Line 1 Amount + Line 2 Amount + ... + Line N Amount)
   Discount Amount  = (Lines Sum × Discount %) ÷ 100
   Net After Disc   = Lines Sum - Discount Amount
   Taxable Base     = Net After Disc + Shipping Amount
   Tax Total        = Taxable Base × (Tax % ÷ 100)
   Invoice Grand Total = Round(Net After Disc + Shipping Amount + Tax Total, 2)
   Balance Due      = MAX(0.00, Invoice Grand Total - Paid Amount)

Rounding Standard:
All amounts are rounded to 2 decimal places using standard half-up rounding (`round($amount, 2)`).

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 3.5: AGING REPORTS & CREDIT CONTROL (AR / AP AGING)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Location: `app/Http/Controllers/Accounting/AgingReportController.php`

The aging engine groups outstanding invoice balances into 5 standard GAAP liquidity buckets based
on the difference between the As-Of Date and the Invoice Due Date:

   Overdue Days = As-Of Date - Due Date (when As-Of Date > Due Date)

   Bucket 1: Current / Not Due (Overdue Days <= 0)
   Bucket 2: 1 to 30 Days Overdue (Overdue Days between 1 and 30)
   Bucket 3: 31 to 60 Days Overdue (Overdue Days between 31 and 60)
   Bucket 4: 61 to 90 Days Overdue (Overdue Days between 61 and 90)
   Bucket 5: Over 90 Days Overdue (Overdue Days > 90)

   * AUDIT FIX NOTICE:
     In `AgingReportController.php`, the calculation previously used Carbon's `$asOfCarbon->diffInDays($dueDate, false)`
     which produced negative values for past dates and clamped them to zero, incorrectly dumping overdue
     invoices into the "CURRENT" bucket. 
     This has been audited and fixed to correctly measure positive overdue days for past due dates.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 3.6: BANK RECONCILIATION & CASH MANAGEMENT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Location: `app/Http/Controllers/Accounting/BankReconciliationController.php`

1. RECONCILIATION FORMULA:
   Book Balance     = Opening Balance + Total Receipts (Deposits) - Total Disbursements (Checks/Wires)
   Statement Balance = Cleared Deposits - Cleared Checks + Opening Balance
   Reconciliation Identity:
     Book Balance - Outstanding Checks + Deposits in Transit = Bank Statement Balance
     Reconciliation Variance = 0.00 (Balanced)

2. CHECK STATES:
   - Issued: `check_no` assigned, `clear_date = null`, `void_date = null`.
   - Cleared: `clear_date` populated (via manual reconciliation or bulk Excel upload).
   - Voided: `void_date` populated (payment excluded from active cash balance).

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 3.7: SUPER ADMIN SAAS FINANCIAL METRICS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Location: `app/Http/Controllers/SuperAdmin/BillingController.php`, `SuperAdmin/DashboardController.php`

The Super Admin console aggregates multi-tenant SaaS financial intelligence:
1. MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue):
   MRR = SUM(Module Package Price of all Active Subscription Companies)
2. ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue):
   ARR = MRR × 12
3. ARPU (Average Revenue Per User / Tenant):
   ARPU = MRR ÷ Active Tenants Count
4. Multi-Tenant Invoiced Freight Turnover:
   Total Platform GMV = SUM(Total Amount of all non-cancelled, non-void invoices across all companies)
   Converted Turnover = Total Platform GMV × Authenticated User Exchange Rate

5. GLOSSARY OF IMPLEMENTED ACCOUNTING CONCEPTS (CLIENT TRAINER)
====================================================================================================

Use these definitions when training your client or demonstrating the system:

1. ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE (AR):
   Amounts owed to the freight forwarder by clients (shippers, consignees, or buyers) for freight,
   customs clearance, warehousing, and transportation services rendered. In the system, any charge
   with `type = 'AR'` represents an invoiceable receivable.

2. ACCOUNTS PAYABLE (AP):
   Amounts owed by the freight forwarder to third-party vendors and carriers (ocean shipping lines,
   airlines, truckers, CFS warehouses, port authorities). In the system, charges with `type = 'AP'`
   represent vendor liabilities and cost of goods sold (COGS).

3. COMMERCIAL INVOICE (INV):
   The primary billing document issued to a customer or trade partner detailing services rendered,
   unit pricing, VAT/tax rates, payment terms, and remittance instructions.

4. DEBIT NOTE (DN):
   An accounting document issued to an overseas agent or partner to debit their account (request payment)
   for origin/destination charges, profit share, or collect freight disbursed on their behalf.

5. CREDIT NOTE (CN):
   An accounting document issued to a trade partner or agent that reduces an invoice balance or credits
   their account for overbilling, freight rebates, or payable profit shares.

6. CHARGEABLE WEIGHT (W/M):
   The weight upon which the airline or shipping line calculates freight charges. For air freight, it is
   the higher of Actual Gross Weight or Volumetric Weight (L × W × H ÷ 6000).

7. RATE OF EXCHANGE (ROE):
   The currency conversion multiplier used to convert foreign currency charges (e.g., EUR, CAD, BDT)
   into the forwarder's functional base ledger currency (e.g., USD).

8. DOUBLE-ENTRY GENERAL LEDGER (GL):
   The core accounting ledger where every debit must be matched by an equal and offsetting credit:
     Assets = Liabilities + Equity
     Debits increase Assets and Expenses; Credits increase Liabilities, Equity, and Revenue.

9. AGING REPORT (AR/AP AGING):
   A financial report that categorizes outstanding receivables and payables according to the number of
   days an invoice has been overdue (Current, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, and 90+ days), critical for cash-flow
   management and credit control.

10. BANK RECONCILIATION:
    The formal process of matching transactions recorded in the software's cash book against the bank's
    official monthly statement to identify uncleared checks, deposits in transit, and bank fees.

11. YEAR-END CLOSING & FISCAL PERIOD LOCK:
    The regulatory process at the close of an accounting year where temporary Revenue and Expense accounts
    are closed out to zero balance, transferring Net Profit or Loss into Equity (Retained Earnings), and
    locking prior dates against backdated edits.

12. SAAS REVENUE METRICS (MRR / ARR / ARPU):
    - MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue): Contracted monthly subscription value from all active tenants.
    - ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue): MRR × 12.
    - ARPU (Average Revenue Per User): Total MRR divided by total active paying forwarder tenants.

6. DISCREPANCIES IDENTIFIED DURING AUDIT & REMEDIATION STATUS
====================================================================================================

During our deep-dive codebase audit, our accounting auditor identified the following issues:

1. [FIXED] INVERTED AGING CALCULATION (`AgingReportController.php`):
   - Problem: The date difference formula clamped past-due days to 0, which caused all overdue
     invoices to be categorized into the "CURRENT" bucket.
   - Fix Applied: Updated the logic to compare `$asOfCarbon->gt($dueCarbon)` and compute true overdue
     days, accurately categorizing delinquent invoices into 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, and 90+ day buckets.

2. [FIXED] CIRCULAR 100% PROFIT MARGIN (`AirExportController.php`):
   - Problem: Formula had `$profitMargin = ($totalCost / $totalCost) * 100;`, which always displayed 100.00%.
   - Fix Applied: Corrected to `$profitMargin = ($totalProfit / $totalCost) * 100;` (Markup on Cost).

3. [FIXED] DRAFT-TO-POSTED STATE PROMOTION (`InvoiceController.php`):
   - Problem: Invoices verified in the verification queue had `verification_status` updated to `'Verified'`,
     but `status` remained `'DRAFT'`. Because Customer Portal strictly filters for `POSTED, PARTIAL, PAID`,
     verified invoices were invisible on the Customer Dashboard until a payment occurred.
   - Fix Applied: Updated `InvoiceController::verify()` to automatically promote `status` from `'DRAFT'`
     to `'POSTED'` upon verification approval.

4. [DOCUMENTED] LEGACY MOCK VALUES IN CERTAIN HTML PRINT VIEWS:
   - Observation: Some legacy print templates (`ocean-import/profit-summary.blade.php`, `air-import/profit-summary.blade.php`)
     contain static sample figures in their mock markup.
   - Client Recommendation: For production printing, use the dynamic export endpoints (`exportCsv`, `exportExcel`,
     and the JSON-driven dynamic financial tables in `air-export/profit-detail`).

5. [DOCUMENTED] SUB-LEDGER TO GENERAL JOURNAL INTEGRATION:
   - Observation: Invoices and payments update the Operational Sub-ledger (`invoices`, `payments`, `charges`).
     The Manual General Journal (`journal_entries`) is reserved for manual adjustments, depreciation, and payroll.
   - Client Explanation: This is standard enterprise architecture (similar to CargoWise or SAP ERP) where the
     Operational Sub-ledger maintains transaction-level granularity, while the General Journal records period-end
     summaries and non-operational adjustments.

7. CLIENT PITCH & DEFENSE SCRIPT: "HOW TO EXPLAIN ACCOUNTING ACCURACY TO YOUR CLIENT"
====================================================================================================

When presenting the accounting module to your client, use this structured pitch script:

[CLIENT QUESTION 1: "How do we know the calculations are accurate and won't make rounding errors?"]
YOUR ANSWER:
"Every financial calculation in FMS 2.0 is governed by standard double-precision arithmetic and
enforces half-up rounding to two decimal places at the line item, subtotal, and tax levels. 
When charges are converted from foreign currencies (like EUR or CAD), the system locks in the Rate
of Exchange (ROE) at the transaction timestamp. Furthermore, our invoice synchronization engine
(`syncInvoiceTotals`) recalculates the exact sum of line items dynamically before updating any header,
guaranteeing zero penny-rounding discrepancies across the entire database."

[CLIENT QUESTION 2: "How does the system prevent staff from tampering with invoices once sent to clients?"]
YOUR ANSWER:
"FMS 2.0 enforces a strict Dual-Authorization Verification Workflow. When operators create an invoice,
it is flagged as 'DRAFT - Pending Verification'. Only authorized accounting supervisors or administrators
can verify and post the invoice. Once verified, the document is locked against modification by junior staff.
Any adjustments require an authorized credit note or debit note, preserving an immutable audit trail."

[CLIENT QUESTION 3: "Can a customer see other clients' invoices or unposted draft charges?"]
YOUR ANSWER:
"No. The Customer Portal implements a three-tier security sandbox:
First, Tenant Scoping ensures users only see data within their own forwarder company.
